## Materials and reagents

45 RIBBiTR isolates, numbered, on plates
10 Janthinobacterium lividum (J. Liv.) isolates, labeled, on plates
1000uL pipette tips
P1000 micropipette
55x 5mL tubes (in packs of 25)
Rack to hold 5mL tubes
1x 50mL tube
PBS, autoclaved, provided by Prof. Kearns
Sterile inoculation loops, in packs of 25
Sharpie
BSC
70% Ethanol

## Procedure

1. Wipe BSC surface with ethanol

2. Wipe packs of 5mL tubes, tube rack, 50mL tube, PBS, and pack of loops with ethanol before placing in BSC. Wipe gloved hands with ethanol before work in BSC.

3. Aliquot PBS into 50mL tube

4. Add 1mL PBS into each 5mL tube, using P1000 micropipette

5. For each isolate, extract enough to cover loop and inoculate into its own tube with PBS. Swirl until all bacteria is off loop. Use new loop for each isolate. LABEL tube after inoculation!

6. Store isolates in PBS in 4 C deli fridge. They should last up to a week.
