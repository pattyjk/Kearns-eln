## Question

Can we make enough broth with MPs in different concentrations?

## Hypothesis

If done well, the serial dilutions should make 15mL MP broth for each of the MP concentrations 10^8, 10^6, 10^4, and 10^2 beads/mL out of the stock MP available to us.

## Prediction

With enough stock MP, we should be able to do the serial dilutions to prepare MP broths for inoculation.

## Materials and reagents

Stock MP, 10^10 beads/mL, stored in "Specimen bag", provided by Prof. Kearns

ParaFilm
BSC
70% Ethanol

## Procedure

1. Prepare serial dilution table in notebook. Use calculator to aid in arithmetic. Seek approval from professor.

2. Clean BSC with ethanol and prepare to work in it

3. Take one 50mL tube and aliquot Tryptone broth into it.

4. In 2mL Eppendorf tube, prepare 10^9 MP broth with 200uL stock MP and 1.8 mL (1,800 uL) Tryptone broth. Label the tube.

5. In each of the remaining 50mL tubes, add 13.5mL Tryptone broth. Use pipet gun with 10mL serological pipets. Re-aliquot broth from bottle when necessary.

6. Starting with 10^9 MP, perform 10-fold serial dilutions until 10^2 MP is made. Keep all of the MP concentrations from this SD process. Each 50mL tube with 13.5mL broth gets 1.5mL of previous concentration of MP. Use P1000 micropipette. Label tubes.

7. Wrap each MP concentration with Parafilm. Store them in 4 C fridge.

## Deviations from the protocol

I thought of doing 100-fold serial dilutions to prepare MP concentrations until prof. suggested otherwise. Then I realized that doing 10-fold dilutions can be more accurate, and that the concentrations with odd powers can be used to make more of concentrations with even powers if what we prepared was not enough for our isolates.
Otherwise, no deviations from the actual SD.
