# Minimal Media Recipe

- **Date performed:** 2026-10-16
- **Recorded by:** Rebecca Reji (reji.re@northeastern.edu)
- **Project:** Establishing a Co-culture System Between the Frog Microbiome and Batrachochytrium dendrobatidis (Bd)

## Hypothesis

Aim: To prepare minimal media to co-culture Bd and bacterial isolates

## Materials and reagents

MATERIALS
- 1 L graduated cylinder
- 1 L media bottle
- 250 mL beaker
- Balance 
- Weigh boats and spatula
- Hot plate and stir bar
- pH meter
- 0.22 µm syringe filter
- 20 mL syringe
- 50 mL Falcon tube

REAGENTS
- DI water
- D-glucose
- Sodium phosphate dibasic (Na2HPO4)
- Sodium phosphate monobasic (NaH2PO4)
- Ammonium sulfate ((NH4)2SO4)
- Sodium chloride (NaCl)
- Potassium chloride (KCl)
- Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO4)
- Calcium Chloride (CaCl2)
- Mucin

## Procedure

MINIMAL SALT BASE
- Measure out approximately 800 mL of DI water into a beaker with a magnetic stir bar
- Weigh and add the following salts one by one, allowing each to dissolve completely:
  + Na2HPO4: 6 g
  + NaH2PO4: 3 g
  + (NH4)2SO4: 2 g
  + NaCl: 0.5 g
  + KCl: 0.2 g
- Bring the final volume to 988 mL using a graduated cylinder
- Adjust pH to 6.8 - 7.0
- Transfer media to a media bottle and autoclave

40% GLUCOSE
- Weigh 40 g of D-glucose
- Dissolve the 40 g of D-glucose in 100 mL of DI Water in a clean beaker using a stir bar until clear
- Aspirate liquid into a 20 mL syringe
- Attach a 0.22 µm syringe filter securely onto the syringe
- Depress it to pass the liquid through the filter directly into a sterile 50 mL conical tube 
- Repeat until all 100 mL has been filter-sterilized. Keep the tube closed to maintain sterility.

1 M MgSO4
- Weigh 2400 mg MgSO4 and dissolve in 20 mL DI water
- Syringe-filter directly into a sterile 50 mL conical tube

0.1 M CaCl2
- Weigh 294 mg CaCl2 and dissolve in 20 mL DI water
- Syringe-filter directly into a sterile 50 mL conical tube

ASSEMBLING COMPLETE MEDIA
- Ensure the autoclaved minimal salts base (988 mL) has cooled completely to room temperature.
- Add Metal Cofactors: Using a micropipette with sterile tips, add:
  + 1 mL of 1 M MgSO4 stock 
  + 1 mL of 0.1 M CaCl2 stock
- Add Carbon Source: Pour 10 mL of filter-sterilised D-glucose solution into the media bottle.
- Swirl gently to mix. 
- Store at 4°C

## Deviations from the protocol

0.07% MUCIN MINIMAL MEDIA
- Add 0.7 g of Mucin to the minimal salt base before autoclaving

## Observations and results

- Media with mucin would appear cloudy
